FAQ

What does VaR actually measure?
Maximum expected loss at a given confidence level over a specific time period — "99% confident we won't lose more than X in one day."
Why compare parametric and historical VaR?
Parametric is fast but assumes normality. Historical is robust to extreme events. Comparing them validates the findings and exposes where assumptions break down.
What does β = 1.6 mean?
The asset is 60% more volatile than the market. A 10% market move implies roughly a 16% move in that asset.
Why EWMA instead of a simple average?
EWMA weights recent data more heavily, capturing volatility spikes during crises. A simple average treats old and new data equally and misses current conditions.
Why does diversification fail during crises?
Correlations rise sharply — from roughly 0.3–0.5 to 0.7–0.9 — as assets move together under stress, removing much of the diversification benefit.
Can the code be modified?
Yes. The notebook is self-contained with clearly separated sections — parameters, time windows, and assets can all be changed.
