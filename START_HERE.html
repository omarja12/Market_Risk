This project implements a Value-at-Risk (VaR) framework for a Russian investor's diversified portfolio with US and German equity exposure, denominated in Russian rubles. The analysis covers September 2017 to September 2022, encompassing major market events including COVID-19 and geopolitical shocks.

Key components:

  • 60% US equity exposure (NASDAQ 100, β=1.6)
  • 40% German equity exposure (DAX, β=1.3)
  • Dual currency risk (USD/RUB, EUR/RUB)
  • EWMA volatility estimation (λ=0.94)
  • Parametric and Historical VaR methods
