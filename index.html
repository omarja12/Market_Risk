Project Overview

A comprehensive quantitative analysis implementing Value-at-Risk (VaR) framework for a Russian investor's diversified portfolio with US and German equity exposure. The project analyzes 5 years of market data (September 2017 - September 2022) covering major market events including COVID-19 and geopolitical shocks.

What Is Value-at-Risk?

VaR answers a fundamental question in portfolio management: "What is the maximum loss I could face in 1 day at a 99% confidence level?"

Why It Matters

  • Risk Management: Set trading limits and position sizes
  • Regulatory Compliance: Basel III capital requirements
  • Strategic Planning: Capital allocation decisions
  • Stakeholder Communication: Transparent risk disclosure

Key Concepts

Beta (β)

Measures sensitivity to market movements. US stocks β=1.6 (60% more volatile than market), German stocks β=1.3 (30% more volatile).

EWMA Volatility

Dynamic volatility estimation with λ=0.94. Emphasizes recent behavior (6% weight) while maintaining historical context (94% weight).

Parametric VaR

Assumes normally distributed returns. Fast and elegant but can underestimate tail risk during crises.

Historical VaR

Uses actual past behavior. More robust to extreme events but limited by historical data availability.

Project Methodology

Multi-Factor Decomposition

The analysis separates equity risk from currency risk using the additive returns model:

Return(RUB) = Return(Equity) + Return(Forex)

This sophisticated approach allows independent management of each risk component.

Risk Estimation

  • EWMA volatility with smoothing parameter λ=0.94
  • Dynamic covariance matrix estimation
  • Correlation analysis showing crisis breakdown
  • Regime comparison (pre-crisis vs. crisis periods)

VaR Calculation

  • Parametric VaR using normal distribution assumptions
  • Systematic VaR incorporating multi-factor correlations
  • Historical VaR for validation and stress testing
  • 1-day and 10-day horizons at 1% significance level

Real-World Impact

COVID-19 Pandemic (March 2020)

NASDAQ dropped 25%, DAX dropped 35%. Volatility spiked 3-5x. Diversification benefits disappeared—both risks moved together.

Russia-Ukraine Conflict (February 2022)

Equity markets declined ~15%, but currency depreciated 45-50%. For a Russian investor, portfolio value in RUB actually increased due to currency effects overwhelming equity losses.

Key Findings

  • VaR estimates ranged from 2.1% to 3.8% depending on time period
  • Correlations increased from 0.3-0.5 (normal) to 0.7-0.9 (crisis)
  • Parametric VaR underestimated during extreme events
  • Historical VaR captured real tail behavior more accurately
