Comprehensive quantitative analysis implementing Value-at-Risk (VaR) methodology for a multi-currency, multi-asset portfolio. The project analyzes 5 years of market data (September 2017 - September 2022) using EWMA volatility estimation and dual VaR approaches.

Portfolio:

  • 60% US equity (NASDAQ 100, β=1.6)
  • 40% German equity (DAX, β=1.3)
  • Dual currency risk (USD/RUB, EUR/RUB)

Overview

What is VaR?

Value-at-Risk answers: "What's the worst-case loss at 99% confidence?" Used by banks, hedge funds, and insurance companies for risk management and regulatory compliance.

Key Findings:

  • EWMA volatility increased 3-5x during crisis periods
  • Pre-crisis correlations 0.3-0.5, crisis correlations 0.7-0.9
  • 1-day 1% VaR: 2.1% - 3.8% depending on period
  • Historical VaR more accurate during tail events

Market Events Analyzed:

  • COVID-19 (March 2020): 25-35% equity decline, extreme volatility
  • Russia-Ukraine (February 2022): 15% equity decline, 45-50% currency depreciation

Methodology

Risk Decomposition:

Return(RUB) = Return(Equity) + Return(Forex)

Additive model separates market risk from currency risk for independent analysis.

Volatility Estimation:

EWMA with λ = 0.94: Recent observations 6% weight, historical 94% weight. Captures crisis volatility spikes while maintaining stability.

VaR Approaches:

  • Parametric: Assumes normal distribution, fast and elegant
  • Historical: Uses actual past behavior, robust to extreme events
  • Both calculated at 99% confidence level
