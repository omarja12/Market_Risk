What is VaR?

Value-at-Risk answers: "What's the maximum loss at 99% confidence level?"

Example: A $1M portfolio with 1-day 1% VaR of $30K means there's a 99% chance it won't lose more than $30K tomorrow.

Used by:

  • Investment banks - set trading desk limits
  • Hedge funds - size positions within risk budgets
  • Insurance companies - model portfolio risk vs. claims
  • Regulators - enforce Basel III capital requirements

Key Concepts

Beta (β)

Market sensitivity. US stocks β=1.6 (60% more volatile than market). German stocks β=1.3 (30% more volatile).

EWMA Volatility

Dynamic estimation with λ=0.94. Gives 6% weight to recent data, 94% to history. Captures crisis spikes.

Parametric VaR

Assumes normal distribution. Fast and elegant but underestimates tail risk during crises.

Historical VaR

Uses actual past returns. Robust to extreme events but limited by historical data availability.

Project Findings

Volatility:

  • Increased 3-5x during crisis periods
  • Clustering evident around market shocks
  • Mean reversion during recovery

Correlations:

  • Normal times: 0.3-0.5 (diversification works)
  • Crisis times: 0.7-0.9 (diversification breaks down)

VaR Estimates:

  • 1-day 1% Parametric VaR: 2.1% - 3.8%
  • Historical VaR more accurate for tail events
  • 10-day VaR approximately √10 times 1-day

Market Events

COVID-19 (March 2020):

  • NASDAQ down 25%, DAX down 35%
  • Volatility spike 3-5x
  • Diversification collapsed

Russia-Ukraine (February 2022):

  • Equity markets down 15%
  • Currency depreciated 45-50%
  • Portfolio value in RUB increased (currency effect dominated)

Methodology

Risk Decomposition:

Return(RUB) = Return(Equity) + Return(Forex)

This separates market risk from currency risk for independent analysis.

Volatility Estimation:

EWMA formula with λ = 0.94 emphasizes recent observations while maintaining historical stability.

VaR Methods:

  • Parametric: Assumes normal distribution
  • Historical: Uses empirical distribution
  • Both at 99% confidence level
  • Both validated against real data

Data & Scope

Time Period: September 2017 - September 2022 (5 years, 1,254 observations)

Markets:

  • NASDAQ 100 (US equities)

  • USD/RUB (exchange rate)
  • EUR/RUB (exchange rate)

Portfolio:
